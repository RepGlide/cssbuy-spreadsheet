Exact Option Guide

Tell a generic title from the actual color, size, version, quantity, material, or set option.

The title is not the option

A listing title is broad. The option you actually select is specific. Do not mistake one for the other.

Color

The title may name one color while several are selectable. Confirm the exact color you are choosing.

Size

A generic size word is not a specific size. Confirm the exact size label you are selecting.

Version

A title can cover several versions or batches. Confirm which version the option points to.

Quantity

A planned quantity is not a chosen quantity. Confirm the number you will actually submit.

Material and set

Confirm the material where it matters, and whether the option is a single item or a set.

?Open item: is the exact option confirmed, or only the broad title?

A worked pin-down

  1. Title: a bag with a general name.
  2. Option: the specific color and size you select.
  3. Result: the option, not the title, is what you carry forward.
