About This Resource

A decision cascade and specificity cards built around a shared CSSBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a CSSBuy spreadsheet. It offers a decision cascade — category, requirement, current destination, exact option, and conflict — so each lead is narrowed step by step with every conflict kept in view.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official CSSBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by CSSBuy or Google. It is not a store, QC certification, fee calculator, order tracker, or seller rating system.

The decision cascade

Everything here follows five steps: Category (what kind of item), Requirement (what needs confirming), Current destination (what the page shows now), Exact option (the actual color, size, version, quantity, or material), and Conflict (what is missing, mismatched, or unconfirmable).

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Narrow each lead to its exact option with conflicts in view.

Decision example — not a live listing or automated result.
