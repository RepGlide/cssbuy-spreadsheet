Specificity Is Context, Not Proof

More Specific Is Not More Real

A precise option gets you closer to your selection. It does not make the item more authentic or better.

1What specificity gives you

  • A closer match to your chosen option.
  • A clearer picture of what you are selecting.
  • A tighter scope for re-checking.

2What specificity cannot give you

  • Proof that the item is authentic.
  • A guarantee of quality or fit.
  • Any confirmation beyond the option itself.
Rule Override Examples

When the Page Says Something Different

When the table and the page disagree, the answer is to write both down — not to quietly replace one with the other.

  • T

    Title mismatch. The page title differs from the row label. Record both.

  • I

    Image mismatch. The image on the page does not match the row. Record both.

  • P

    Price context. The currency or time differs from the table. Record both.

  • O

    Option mismatch. A color, size, or version is missing or changed. Record both.

No Silent Overrides

Never Quietly Replace One with the Other

The table is one source; the page is another. Neither silently erases the other.

  • 1

    Both stay visible. Record the table value and the page value side by side.

  • 2

    Resolve before continuing. Confirm which is current before you rely on either.

  • 3

    No quiet drops. A mismatch stays in the note until it is settled.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to narrow down, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official CSSBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by CSSBuy or Google.

Does the decision cascade verify products or certify quality?

No. The decision cascade is a narrowing routine. It is not a store, QC certification, fee calculator, order tracker, or seller rating system. A more specific option is closer to your selection, not proof of authenticity or quality.

How do I narrow a lead to an exact option?

Work in order: identify the category, name the requirement, record what the current page shows, then confirm the exact color, size, version, quantity, or material — and note any conflict.

What happens when the table and page conflict?

Write the conflict down. Do not silently replace the table’s information with the page’s. Keep both visible until the mismatch is resolved.

Where do I place an order?

After you have narrowed the lead and recorded any conflicts, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
